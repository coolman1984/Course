The ready tool - unit 3, example 2: the commercial review
=========================================================

One click:
1. Unzip into a folder of its own, and keep the Data and Ready_Tool folders side by side as they are.
2. Open the Ready_Tool folder and double-click Run_Tool.bat.
3. The tool runs 4 steps, and each step writes what it did. At the end you must see the line ALL STEPS PASSED, and
   the deck opens by itself.

What appears in the Output folder:
- Q3_Commercial_Review.xlsx: the tables (Sales with two VLOOKUP columns, Shipments, Returns, Targets, Sources), the
  pivot tables, the report and the charts.
- Q3_Commercial_Review.pptx and a PDF of it: 8 slides, every number read from the workbook.
- Check_Report.txt: the figures that must come out; compare them with Answer_Key.txt.
- Run_Log.txt: the log of every step and every check.

What the laptop needs:
- Excel, Word and PowerPoint (2016 or later) and Windows PowerShell.
- Python 3 with its standard library only, no package.

If the tool stops: read the last lines on the screen or in Output\Run_Log.txt. They give the file and the reason.
The documentation is in the docs folder: SKILL.md, HISTORY.md and LESSONS.md.
